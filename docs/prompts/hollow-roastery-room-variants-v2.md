# Hollow Roastery Room Variants — Prompt Set v2

## Generation mode

`stylized-concept`

The available built-in reference-image pipeline was used with the approved Higgsfield environment specification. Each v2 generation referenced its matching v1 runtime plate to lock camera, arena proportions, rendering quality, and lighting hierarchy while requesting a structurally different room. These files are not represented as direct Higgsfield renders.

## Common locked prompt

Create a final runtime background plate for the original DOFFA Heroes mobile top-down action roguelite.

- Premium stylized 3D environment art with dark, serious coffee-industrial fantasy materials and chunky readable forms.
- Exact 9:16 portrait composition and fixed high 58-degree orthographic three-quarter top-down camera.
- Centered symmetrical arena, sealed exit centered in the top wall, entry threshold centered at the bottom.
- Broad open combat floor from roughly 18% to 90% image height.
- Use the matching v1 reference only to lock camera, arena proportions, polish, and lighting hierarchy; redesign the architecture visibly.
- Blackened iron, scorched brass, dark steel, cracked ivory ceramic, soot, grime, believable wear, and restrained reflections.
- Large machinery only on the upper wall, side walls, corners, and narrow margins; quiet central contrast.
- No character, creature, face, weapon, projectile, UI, text, logo, number, watermark, arrow, loot, coin, bean, central obstacle, loose crate, floor hazard circle, combat telegraph, gameplay marker, isometric dollhouse, side view, or bright cartoon color.

## Variant payloads

### Soot Conveyor — Ash v2

Scorched coffee-roastery conveyor and filtration vault with soot hoppers, sealed filter cabinets, short belt mechanisms, ash collection ducts, blackened iron pipes, burnt brick, and low amber vents placed only at the perimeter. Dim soot-filled atmosphere, dying-furnace highlights, worn masonry, and subtle ash deposits.

### Boiler Gallery — Ember v2

Cracked refractory boiler gallery with two compact kiln mouths integrated into the upper-left and upper-right walls, vertical heat exchangers on the side margins, scorched iron braces, ember-lit vents, and thin floor seams that remain visual texture only. Strong orange furnace light, broad uncluttered center.

### Meter Chamber — Brass v2

Dark precision coffee machinery hall with oversized round pressure meters, crankshafts, flywheels, grinder hoppers, narrow brass service gantries, and ceramic insulators arranged along the perimeter. Cooler brass work lights, charcoal metal-tile playfield, and a richer mechanical silhouette than v1.

### Vapor Crypt — Smoke v2

Cold damp extraction vault with tall condenser stacks, ribbed exhaust ducts, soot-black filter shrines, drain channels flat in the floor texture, pale steam confined to narrow side margins, and restrained ember status lamps. Darkest and coolest district; no center-obscuring fog.

### Final Gauge — Pressure v2

Reinforced high-pressure engineering chamber with paired accumulator tanks, huge perimeter gauge housings, valves, pistons, reinforced copper manifolds, riveted armor plating, small redline status lights, and short steam traces only at the walls. Wide empty central lane and tense late-tour atmosphere.

## Runtime export

Accepted sources were center-cropped to exact 9:16, resized to 720×1280, stripped of metadata, and exported as progressive JPEG at quality 88. The five v2 assets are catalogued beside the six v1 plates in `docs/ROOM_ASSETS.md`.
