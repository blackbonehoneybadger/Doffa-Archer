# Hollow Roastery Runtime Rooms — Prompt Set v1

## Generation mode

`stylized-concept`

The available built-in reference-image pipeline was used with the approved Higgsfield environment specification. Each generation referenced its matching crop from the approved Hollow Roastery overview; the approved Ash Storage runtime plate was then used to lock camera and composition across the remaining rooms. These outputs are not represented as direct Higgsfield renders.

## Common locked prompt

Create a final in-game portrait background plate for an original mobile top-down action roguelite. Use premium stylized 3D game art with slightly chunky readable forms and a serious dark coffee-industrial fantasy tone; do not copy any existing game.

- Exact 9:16 portrait composition.
- Fixed high 58-degree orthographic / three-quarter top-down camera.
- Centered symmetrical arena, sealed exit centered in the top wall, entry centered at the bottom.
- Keep the combat floor from approximately 18% to 90% of image height broad, open, uncluttered, and readable for 360-degree movement, enemies, bullets, hazards, and touch controls.
- Confine large machinery to outer walls, upper back wall, corners, and narrow side margins.
- Use blackened iron, scorched brass, dark steel, cracked ivory ceramic, believable soot, grime, steam, and restrained warm reflections.
- Preserve strong atmosphere and perimeter detail while keeping contrast quiet in the live playfield.
- No boss, human, face, character, monster, creature, weapon, projectile, combat effect, health bar, joystick, HUD, UI, text, logo, number, watermark, arrow, loot, coin, bean, label, split screen, or dollhouse view.
- No pre-painted collision props, loose central obstacles, floor hazard circles, damage telegraphs, or gameplay markers.

## Room payloads

### Ash Storage

Scorched brick storage vault with ash drifts, sealed black-metal bins, small pressure pipes, and low amber vents around the perimeter. Warm brown masonry floor, dusty air, restrained dying-furnace light, and the cleanest/least advanced machinery of the tour.

### Cracked Furnace

Cracked refractory floor and furnace district with one active kiln integrated into the upper wall. Ember light shows through deep floor seams without painting gameplay hazards. Side vents, heat haze, scorched brick, iron braces, and stronger orange light than Ash Storage.

### Grinder Hall

Dark precision workshop with industrial coffee grinders, flywheels, hoppers, gears, and brass service cabinets arranged only along both walls. Quiet charcoal square-tile playfield, symmetrical mechanical density, and cooler brass work-lighting.

### Steam Chamber

Cold charcoal tile room with extractor hoods, condensers, ducts, and steam outlets confined to the edges. Pale steam curls down the side margins without obscuring the center. Cooler, darker, damp atmosphere with restrained ember accents.

### Pressure Works

Heavy riveted metal-plate floor surrounded by dense pressure tanks, gauges, valves, pistons, and reinforced pipes. Symmetric high-pressure engineering language, narrow steam plumes near walls, warm status lights, and a wide empty central lane.

### Roaster Heart

Final boss chamber with giant circular pressure-reactor architecture integrated into the upper back wall and outer ring only; the reactor is architecture, never a creature. Deep ember-orange light glows through edge vents and seams. Subtle concentric brass inlays and roasting sigils may sit flat in the open ceremonial boss floor. The central zone must remain empty for the oversized animated boss.

## Runtime export

All accepted sources were center-cropped to 9:16, resized to 720×1280, stripped of metadata, and exported as progressive JPEG at quality 88. The exact output mapping is documented in `docs/ROOM_ASSETS.md`.
